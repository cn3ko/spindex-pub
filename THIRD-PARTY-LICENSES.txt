SpinDex — Third-Party Components and Licenses
==============================================

SpinDex itself is licensed under the MIT License (see LICENSE). That
governs SpinDex's own code only — it says nothing about what SpinDex is
built from, which is what the rest of this file covers.

The guiding rule for this installer: **SpinDex distributes only its own
binaries and permissively-licensed components** (plus a few unmodified
MPL-2.0 libraries, listed below). Nothing under the GPL,
LGPL or CC BY-SA is shipped inside it. Components with those
terms are fetched from their own publishers onto your machine, by you, when
you choose to enable the features that need them.

PART 1 — SHIPPED IN THE INSTALLER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Machine-learning models (data files, loaded at analysis time)

  Beat This!  (CPJKU / Johannes Kepler University Linz, ISMIR 2024)
    Purpose : beat and tempo (BPM) detection
    License : MIT — stated explicitly in the repo: "The code and the
              published model weights are released under the MIT license."
    Source  : https://github.com/CPJKU/beat_this
    Note    : the bundled file is an ONNX export of the official checkpoint,
              obtained from https://github.com/mosynthkey/beat_this_cpp (MIT
              for that repo's own code; that repo explicitly defers to
              CPJKU's terms for the model itself rather than asserting its
              own — verified 2026-08-26, no separate claim found).
    Caveat  : CPJKU's own repo also states "some of the training files are
              fully copyrighted or under limited Creative Commons licenses,
              and it is up to the user to assess whether this may impact
              their use case." That is the authors disclosing that the
              model's training-data provenance is not fully clean and
              explicitly leaving that assessment to downstream users. An
              MIT license on the resulting weights governs redistribution
              of those weights; it does not resolve whether training on
              that corpus was itself permissible, which is an unsettled
              question industry-wide, not something this project can close.

  OpenKeyScan v3  (rekordcloud)
    Purpose : musical key detection, reported in Camelot notation
    License : MIT (repo-wide LICENSE file) — weaker confidence than Beat
              This! above: the repo has no explicit statement that the MIT
              grant covers the .pt checkpoint specifically, only a generic
              LICENSE file covering the repository. Treated here as MIT by
              the conventional reading (a repo-wide LICENSE covers
              everything in it absent a stated exception), not because the
              authors said so directly the way CPJKU did — verified
              2026-08-26.
    Source  : https://github.com/rekordcloud/openkeyscan-analyzer
    Based on: MusicalKeyCNN by a1ex90 (MIT),
              https://github.com/a1ex90/MusicalKeyCNN — OpenKeyScan states it
              "is based on and extends the original MusicalKeyCNN
              implementation"
    Note    : the bundled `spindex-key.onnx` is an ONNX conversion of the
              published `openkeyscan3.pt` checkpoint, produced by
              scripts/convert-key-model.py in this repository

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2 — NOT SHIPPED: OPTIONAL COMPONENTS YOU INSTALL YOURSELF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

These are downloaded from their own publishers by
`install-components.ps1` (ffmpeg) or `setup-apollo.ps1` (Apollo).
SpinDex neither redistributes them nor relicenses them; each remains the
property of its authors under its own terms, and SpinDex works without them.

  FFmpeg
    Purpose : audio decoding (mp3/flac/aiff/… -> raw PCM) for analysis and
              quality measurement, aiff transcoding for playback, and the
              audio written by Enhance / Steady tempo / Restore
    License : GNU General Public License v3 (the gyan.dev "essentials" build
              is configured with --enable-gpl --enable-version3)
    Source  : https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
              https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/  (build recipes and source)
    Notes   : SpinDex invokes ffmpeg.exe as a separate process and does not
              link against it. Because it is not distributed with SpinDex, the
              GPLv3 attaches only to the copy on your own machine.

  Apollo  (JusperLee, "Apollo: Band-sequence Modeling for High-Quality Music
          Restoration in Compressed Audio")
    Purpose : optional ML restoration of audio damaged by heavy lossy
              compression
    License : Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
              (CC BY-SA 4.0) — a share-alike license, which is exactly why it
              is never bundled
    Source  : https://github.com/JusperLee/Apollo
              https://huggingface.co/JusperLee/Apollo  (model weights)
    Notes   : installed by scripts/setup-apollo.ps1 together with a private
              Python environment (PyTorch, BSD-3-Clause). The Restore feature
              stays hidden in the UI until those files exist.

The full text of the GPLv3 is at https://www.gnu.org/licenses/ , and
of CC BY-SA 4.0 at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ .

PART 3 — ONLINE SERVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Used only by the optional, explicitly user-invoked "Fetch online" action in
the metadata editor. No account or key is required and nothing is sent but a
text query (the track's artist and title).

  MusicBrainz  https://musicbrainz.org/    (open data, CC0 / CC BY-NC-SA
                                            depending on the dataset)
