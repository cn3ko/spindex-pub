Privacy

SpinDex doesn't send anything anywhere. No account, no sign-up, no tracking, no analytics — it never talks to the internet unless you tell it to. The one exception: if you press the optional "Fetch online" button while editing a track's tags, SpinDex sends the artist and title of that one song as a text search to a public music database (MusicBrainz) to help identify it — nothing else, and only when you ask.

FAQ
Is SpinDex free?

Yes, completely — no trial, no subscription, no paid tiers.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC" — is this safe?

That's SmartScreen warning about any app that isn't code-signed (a paid certificate SpinDex doesn't have yet). It doesn't mean anything is wrong — click More info → Run anyway.

Does SpinDex need the internet?

No. Scanning, searching, editing tags, finding duplicates and managing crates all work with zero internet connection. The only thing that needs it is the optional "Fetch online" button, and it only runs when you press it.

Will SpinDex change or delete my music files?

Never without you asking. Tag edits only happen when you hit Save. Deleting a track sends it to the Recycle Bin (or its USB equivalent), never a permanent delete. Your files stay the source of truth — the app's own database can be deleted and rebuilt any time with zero data loss.

Does it work with Serato or Rekordbox?

Yes for Serato — SpinDex can read and edit your Serato crates directly, with automatic backups before every change. Rekordbox support is read-only for now: SpinDex shows your Rekordbox playlists in the sidebar but doesn't edit them.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

No. SpinDex is Windows only, and there are no plans for macOS or Linux versions.

Who makes SpinDex?

SpinDex is a hobby project by one person, built in spare time. It isn't a company or a commercial product: nothing is sold, there are no ads, and there's no paid version. That also means there's no guaranteed support and no warranty. It's offered as-is, and updates come when they come.
