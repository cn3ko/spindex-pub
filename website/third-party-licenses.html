Third-party components and licences

SpinDex itself is MIT-licensed — see its licence. That governs SpinDex's own code only; it says nothing about what SpinDex is built from, which is what the rest of this page covers.

The guiding rule for the installer: SpinDex ships only its own binaries and permissively-licensed components (plus a few unmodified MPL-2.0 libraries, listed below). Nothing under the GPL, LGPL or CC BY-SA is shipped inside it — those are fetched from their own publishers, onto your own machine, only when you choose to enable the features that need them.

Shipped in the installer — machine-learning models

Beat This!

CPJKU / Johannes Kepler University Linz — ISMIR 2024
Purpose
Beat and tempo (BPM) detection
Licence
MIT — stated explicitly in the repo: “the code and the published model weights are released under the MIT license”
Source
github.com/CPJKU/beat_this

The bundled file is an ONNX export of the official checkpoint, obtained from mosynthkey/beat_this_cpp — that repo's own code is MIT too, but it explicitly defers to the original for the model's own terms rather than asserting separate rights over it.

Worth knowing: the same repo also states that “some of the training files are fully copyrighted or under limited Creative Commons licenses, and it is up to the user to assess whether this may impact their use case.” That's the authors themselves flagging that the model's training data has murky provenance. A permissive licence on the resulting weights doesn't resolve that — it's an open question across the ML field generally, not something this page can close out on your behalf.

Not shipped — you install these yourself

Downloaded from their own publishers by install-components.ps1 (ffmpeg) or setup-apollo.ps1 (Apollo). SpinDex neither redistributes nor relicenses them — each stays the property of its authors under its own terms, and SpinDex works without them.

FFmpeg

Purpose
Audio decoding for analysis and quality measurement, aiff transcoding for playback, and the audio written by Enhance / Steady tempo / Restore
Licence
GNU General Public License v3
Source
ffmpeg.org

SpinDex invokes ffmpeg.exe as a separate process and doesn't link against it — since it's never distributed with SpinDex, the GPLv3 applies only to the copy on your own machine.

Apollo

JusperLee — “Apollo: Band-sequence Modeling for High-Quality Music Restoration in Compressed Audio”
Purpose
Optional ML restoration of audio damaged by heavy lossy compression
Licence
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0) — a share-alike licence, which is exactly why it's never bundled
Source
github.com/JusperLee/Apollo, weights on Hugging Face

Installed by setup-apollo.ps1 alongside a private Python environment (PyTorch, BSD-3-Clause). The Restore feature stays hidden until those files exist.

Worth knowing: Creative Commons itself recommends against using CC licences for software, since they weren't drafted with linking or binary-distribution mechanics in mind — that's Apollo's author's choice, not something this project can smooth over. Spawning it as a separate process rather than incorporating its code is the standard, reasonable defensive argument for why share-alike doesn't attach to SpinDex itself — but it's an untested argument, not a legally settled one.

Full text: GPLv3 · CC BY-SA 4.0
